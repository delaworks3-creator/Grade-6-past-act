My Sunday

Last Sunday, I woke up at seven o’clock. I helped my mother in the kitchen. Then, I cleaned my bedroom. In the afternoon, I played badminton with my friends. We were very happy. At night, I watched a movie with my family.
1. When did the writer wake up?
2. Who did the writer help?
3. What did the writer clean?
4. What did the writer do in the afternoon?
5. Who watched a movie with the writer?
